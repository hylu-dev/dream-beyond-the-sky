Generated with the built-in image generation tool, editing the previous

not installed in the game. Outer glow still requires production cleanup.

## Prompt

Edit the attached cloud concept into a much simpler, deliberately hand-painted GAME ENVIRONMENT CLOUD SPRITE. The current image is too intricately detailed, too luminous, and has an overprocessed AI fantasy appearance.

Retain the general asymmetric cloud silhouette, muted blue-grey shadows and warm cream light from the upper right, but REDUCE DETAIL DRASTICALLY:
- Organize the entire cloud into roughly 7 to 10 large, clearly designed overlapping billows.
- Use broad calm gouache color shapes and restrained soft transitions, with only a few visible brush marks.
- Remove all tiny cauliflower curls, filigree, fractal outlines, granular noise, sparkling edges and repetitive micro-billows.
- Simplify the shadow into two or three connected blue-grey masses.
- Limit the palette to about five closely related muted paint colors.
- The light is gentle warm daylight, not glowing backlight. NO bloom, aura, luminous rim, rays or overexposure.
- Mix a few firm painted edges with a few softly lost edges. Keep the silhouette irregular and intentional, not a row of circles, scalloped sticker, vector icon, smooth plastic or cartoon emoji.

Aim for an understated animation background painting study, economical and readable at small size, with a natural handmade feeling. Leave broad quiet areas of nearly solid color. This is not a photorealistic cloud or a fantasy spectacle.

Production framing: ONE cloud isolated on actual transparent background. Pull back so the complete shape occupies about 70 percent of the canvas width, with clear transparent padding on every side. Entire body opaque including shadows, transparency only at a few soft wispy edges. No background, no ground shadow, no text, no frame.
